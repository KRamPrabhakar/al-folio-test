## Biography

I am a Assistant Professor at Wadhwani School of Data Science & Artificial Intelligence, IIT Madras. Previously, I worked as a Assistant Research Scientist under the guidance of [Prof. Rama Chellappa](https://engineering.jhu.edu/ece/faculty/rama-chellappa/) at Johns Hopkins University. I obtained my Ph.D. from the Video Analytics Lab at the [Computational and Data Sciences](http://cds.iisc.ac.in/) department of the Indian Institute of Science, where I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. R. Venkatesh babu](http://cds.iisc.ac.in/faculty/venky/). My research primarily focuses on computer vision, computational photography, with a particular interest in long video understanding, VLM compositional reasoning and 3D computer vision. For a comprehensive overview of my work, please visit the [Research](portfolio/) and [Publications](publications/) page.

I am an **Assistant Professor** in the [Wadhwani School of Data Science & Artificial Intelligence](https://wsai.iitm.ac.in/) at **IIT Madras**. 

Prior to joining IIT Madras, I was an Assistant Research Scientist at Johns Hopkins University, working under the guidance of [Prof. Rama Chellappa](https://engineering.jhu.edu/ece/faculty/rama-chellappa/). I earned my Ph.D. from the Vision and AI Lab in the Department of [Computational and Data Sciences](http://cds.iisc.ac.in/) at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu](http://cds.iisc.ac.in/faculty/venky/).

### Research Interests

My research focuses broadly on computer vision and visual intelligence, with specific interests in:
* Long Video Understanding & Video-Language Models
* VLM Compositional Reasoning
* 3D Computer Vision & Computational Photography

For a complete overview of my ongoing projects and academic output, please explore my [Research](/portfolio/) and [Publications](/publications/) pages.
