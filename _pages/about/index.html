Assistant Professor, Department of Data Science and AI at IIT Madras

Leading the Artificial Perception X (APX) Lab, working on computer vision, deep learning, image processing, and machine learning.

Previously, I was an Assistant Research Scientist under the guidance of Dr. Rama Chellappa at Johns Hopkins University. I received my Ph.D. from Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Bangalore, advised by Dr. Venkatesh Babu.

My research focuses on building machines that can perceive, understand, and reason about the visual world - developing methods that are robust, efficient, and generalizable across diverse real-world conditions.
