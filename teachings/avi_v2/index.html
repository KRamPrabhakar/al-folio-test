About

This course covers fundamental and advanced topics in computer vision and visual intelligence. Students will develop a strong understanding of both classical methods and modern deep learning approaches, with emphasis on mathematical foundations and practical implementation.

Topics include image formation, feature extraction, object detection and segmentation, generative models, video understanding, and vision-language models.
