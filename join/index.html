  • PhD and MS by Research

    I am looking for 2 strong, self-motivated, fully-funded PhD/MS students to join APX Lab.

    How to apply: Admission is through the official IITM Research Admissions portal.

    Eligibility shortcuts:

    • GPA > 8 from IITs/NITs → GATE not required
    • MS/MTech/ME degree holders → GATE not required
    • See official handbook for full details

    Deadlines:

    • July start → apply by March
    • January start → apply by October

    After applying, email me with your application ID, CV, and a brief note on your research interests.

  • Writing to Me

    I receive many emails and may not respond to all of them — I apologize in advance.

    To improve your chances of a response, please include:

    1. Interests and goals — what draws you to this work
    2. CV (PDF)
    3. Research statement (1–2 paragraphs) — prior projects in ML/CV/AI. Write in your own words. AI-generated text is easy to spot and will work against you.
    4. Transcripts (unofficial is fine)
    5. Timeline — start date and duration
    6. Something specific from our work — a genuine insight from reading our papers. Superficial engagement is obvious.

    If you do not hear back, one follow-up after 1–2 weeks is welcome.
