PhD and MS by Research

I am looking for 2 strong, self-motivated, fully-funded PhD/MS students to join APX Lab.

Admission is through the official IITM Research Admissions portal.

You may be exempt from GATE if:

  • GPA > 8 from IITs or NITs
  • You hold an MS/MTech/ME degree from India or abroad

See the official handbook for full eligibility details.

Application deadlines:

  • July (fall) start → apply by March
  • January (spring) start → apply by October

Once you have applied, email me with your application ID, CV, and a brief note on your research background and interests.

I typically take 1–2 Post-Bacc fellows per year.

Postdoctoral Fellowship

I welcome applications from candidates with a PhD and strong publication record at top venues (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR).

What we offer:

  • Self-directed research
  • Mentorship for faculty/industry careers
  • Conference travel support
  • Access to compute resources

Reach out via email with your CV, research statement, and 2–3 representative publications.

Writing to Me

I receive many emails and may not respond to all of them - I apologize in advance.

To improve your chances of a response, please include:

  1. Interests and goals - what draws you to this work
  2. CV (PDF)
  3. With subject line: [Prospective <PhD/MS/BTP/Intern/PA/Postdoc>] Your Name
  4. Research statement (1–2 paragraphs) - prior projects in ML/CV/AI. Write in your own words. AI-generated text is easy to spot.
  5. Timeline - start date and duration

If you do not hear back, one follow-up after 1–2 weeks is welcome.
